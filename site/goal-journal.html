The Goal Journal

A goal journal laid out like a shelf of notebooks, with an AI panel called The Mirror that reads your entries and shows you the patterns in your own writing.

Role
Solo build
Stack
Next.js, TypeScript, Prisma, NextAuth, Vercel AI SDK, Claude and Gemini APIs
Links
CodeRésumé (Software Engineer, AI Code Review)

The problem

The project starts from one line: life can only be lived forward but only understood backwards. People write about their goals every day but rarely reread what they wrote, so the patterns in their own entries stay invisible to them.

What I built

A journaling app with one notebook per goal, and an AI that reads the notebook back to you.

  • →A notebook for each goal on a shelf, with a lined page for each day and a short template: what I did, what worked, what did not, and tomorrow
  • →The Mirror, a panel that slides in beside the page, reads every entry in the journal and returns patterns, gaps and one specific next action, with a chat for follow-up questions
  • →Scanning of handwritten pages: a photo of a paper journal is read with Claude vision and turned into dated entries, checked against a Zod schema
  • →Sign-in with NextAuth and a Prisma data model for users, journals and entries

How it works

The Mirror is designed not to coach: it only reflects what is already in the writing. Its readings are cached for each journal, so reopening the panel does not trigger a new AI call every time. The AI provider is a single setting, so the same code can run on Gemini, Claude or OpenAI.

Outcome

3

AI providers behind one setting

4

prompts in every daily entry

1

specific next action per reading

I wrote a detailed spec for The Mirror first, covering layout, interactions and where it connects to the app, then built it with Claude Code in focused steps and checked each step against the spec.
