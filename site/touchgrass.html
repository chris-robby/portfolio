03 / Automated website

touchgrass

Free and $10-and-under things to do in Montreal and Ottawa, rebuilt automatically every morning from public sources.

The problem

Free events exist all over Montreal and Ottawa, but they are scattered across city datasets and university calendars. Finding something to do tonight means checking many sites, and paid events often do not state a price.

What I built

A bilingual site that gathers those events in one place, with several ways to browse them.

  • →List, calendar and map views, with filters for date, category, audience and borough
  • →A swipe-style Discover page for browsing on a phone
  • →Separate pages and data for each city, in French and English
  • →A strict price rule: an event only appears if it is free or its stated price is $10 or under

How it works

The most important decision was what happens when something breaks, because nobody is watching at 5 a.m. Any failed download, empty result, sharp drop in event count, failed test or failed build stops the deployment, and the live site keeps the previous day's version.

# Rebuilds the event data and the site every morning, then deploys to GitHub Pages.
#
# If any step fails (download error, zero events, count dropped by half, tests,
# build), nothing is deployed and the live site keeps yesterday's version.
# GitHub emails the repo owner when a scheduled run fails.
name: Daily build & deploy

on:
  schedule:
    # 10:00 UTC = 5–6 a.m. in Montréal. The city refreshes its dataset around midnight.
    - cron: "0 10 * * *"

From .github/workflows/daily.yml
