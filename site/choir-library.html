02 / Real-world web app

Digital library for a choir

A shared home for a choir's scores and audio that also tracks every member's contribution, replacing manual tracking.

Role
Developer
Stack
Vue.js, Supabase, REST APIs
Links
Résumé (Software Developer)

The problem

A choir of more than 30 members needs its music scores and audio in one place, and its organizers need to know where each member's contribution stands. Doing that by hand takes time and is hard to keep consistent.

What I built

A web application with one view for members and another for the people running the choir.

  • →A member view for finding music scores and audio
  • →An admin overview, laid out like a spreadsheet, showing every member's contribution at once
  • →Contribution tracking for each member, replacing the manual process

How it works

Instead of one application, I split the system into four services that deploy independently and communicate over REST: authentication, storage, the admin view and the members view.

Outcome

30+

members using it

4

independently deployed services

The choir saved time and gained efficiency, consistency and a stronger sense of unity. Building for people I know also meant the tool had to work for them day to day.
